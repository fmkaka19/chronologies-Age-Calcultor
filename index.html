How Chronological Age Is Calculated

Chronological age represents the exact duration of time a person has lived from their birth date up to a target "As Of" date. Unlike simple calendar year subtraction, accurate age calculation measures full completed years, remaining full months, and leftover days. It accounts for variable month lengths (28, 30, or 31 days) and leap years (February 29) to ensure precise day-by-day accuracy.
